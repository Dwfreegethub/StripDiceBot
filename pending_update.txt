Pick lists now show which items will actually appear on the player you're binding.
Some BC restraints only have artwork for certain bodies — a straitjacket, most harnesses and every corset need breasts, chastity belts need a vagina. Applied to a body they don't fit, they still count as one of your seven items but nothing shows up, which is how people ended up "bound" and looking untouched.
When you're picking, items that can't appear on that person now sink to the bottom of the list under a divider, with the reason next to them (needs breasts, needs a vagina). They're still pickable if you want them. Slots where nothing at all would show are flagged in the slot list, so you don't spend your turn on a dead end.
This reads the body the character is actually wearing, not their pronouns, so it's right for every combination.
A batch of items in the catalogue turned out not to exist in this version of BC at all — those are now marked too, instead of quietly ranking near the top.
