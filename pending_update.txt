Tidied up the whispers you get on walking in — one message now instead of three.
Walking into the room used to fire three whispers back to back: a welcome, a matchmaking nudge, and a "there's been an update" nudge. They're one message now, and it says what the room is up to, all three ways to play on a single line (!join for multiplayer, !teamgame for 2v2 or 3v3, !solo race / !solo survive on your own), and anything new.
The matchmaking line only shows if you haven't joined the pool yet — once you've whispered !bd register it stops nagging you, and you just get a "whisper !looking" note when you turn up to an empty room.
Nothing about how any of the games work has changed.
