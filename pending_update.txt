🔔 New: whisper !bd register, then !looking beeps everyone online when you want a game.
Matchmaking pool — nobody should have to sit in an empty room hoping someone turns up. Whisper !bd register once (you'll add me as a friend too, which is how beeps reach you), and from then on you get a beep whenever someone is here looking for a game.
When YOU are the one waiting, whisper !looking and I'll beep every registered player who's online right now, telling them you're here. If any of them reply to that beep, I pass their reply straight back to you.
!bd pause and !bd resume turn the beeps off and on without leaving the pool; !bd unregister leaves it entirely; !bd status shows where you stand. Whisper !bd for the full list.
Two house rules: !looking has a 30-minute cooldown so the pool doesn't get spammed, and please stay in the room for at least 3 minutes after calling it — people need time to see the beep and travel over. Walking straight back out after calling wastes everyone's beep, and doing it repeatedly drops you from the pool.
Also on this update: tournament mode is installed but dormant. Unless an admin actually starts one, nothing about the room, your games, or your commands behaves differently — !tournament only appears in the help while a tournament is genuinely running. A small test tournament with volunteers comes first, on very short rounds. Format when it does run: Swiss pairing with double elimination — each round you're paired with someone on a similar record, you each play solo Survive games on your own time, most rolls survived wins, lose twice and you're out.
