# Safety

## The safeword

**`/bot safeword`** (or `!safeword`) removes all your restraints straight away, at any point in a game. The Bondage Club safeword does the same.

* In a multiplayer game, the game pauses and asks the others whether to carry on without you (`/bot continue` within 60 seconds). Otherwise the game ends.
* In a team game, the game carries on and your turns roll automatically (see [ghosts](Team-Mode#ghosts)).

Nobody will be cross with you for using it.

## Pausing

`/bot pause` asks for a quick pause during a turn: for a drink, the door, or a breather. Anyone can `/bot resume`.

## What you can say no to

| When | How |
|---|---|
| Toys at the end of the game | Say no to the toy question. With any no, there are no toys that game. |
| Being a prize | Say no to the prize question, or don't answer. |
| Body areas in pick mode | Leave them out of your list. Nipples, Breast and Pelvis are off unless you name them. |
| A restraint someone picked | `/bot veto` within 30 seconds. |
| Restraints chosen by other players at all | Answer **outfit** to the outfit-or-pick question. |

## Locks that didn't release

When your lock time runs out, the bot asks you to confirm. Type `/bot stuck` if anything is still locked, and an admin will get you out. Admins can also free a player at any time.

## Telling us

Something felt wrong, or a rule should change? Type `/bot feedback` and say it. Every message is read.
