# FAQ

**I typed `/bot roll` and nothing happened.**
Check it's your turn: the bot says whose turn it is in the room. If you lost your last roll, take the item off first (close your Wardrobe, or `/bot removed`). If the bot doesn't know a command it says so, so no reply at all may mean the bot is restarting.

**Do the `!` commands still work?**
Yes, every one. `/bot` just keeps the room quieter. Commands typed with `!` in room chat are seen by everyone; whispered ones aren't.

**The bot asked me a question. How do I answer?**
Whisper the answer to the bot (`yes`, `no`, `2`...), or type `/bot yes`.

**I can't join the game.**
Games can only be joined before they start, unless an admin has turned on mid-game joining. Between games there's a 5-minute break. A [solo game](Solo-Games) is always open.

**Something was put on me that doesn't show on my character.**
Some Bondage Club items only have artwork for certain bodies. The picker sees a warning for these, but they can still be chosen. Tell us with `/bot feedback` if it keeps happening.

**My lock didn't come off.**
Type `/bot stuck`. An admin will free you.

**Which version is running?**
The bot's "online" message shows it, and so does `/bot about`.

**I have an idea.**
`/bot feedback` and your idea, or `/bot outfit` and a description to suggest a bondage outfit.
