# Solo Games

A private game against the record books. Start one any time, even alone in the room. Everything happens by whisper.

## Starting

* `/bot solo race` — **fewest** rolls to get naked wins.
* `/bot solo survive` — **most** rolls before you're naked wins.

The bot asks about your outfit item by item, or offers last time's. You need **3 to 7 items**. Your item count decides which record table you play against, so scores are only compared with people wearing the same number of items.

## Playing

* `/bot roll` each time. The die starts at D100 and shrinks to your last roll.
* A 1 means take off an item. Close your Wardrobe (or `/bot removed`) and carry on. There's no rush.
* The game ends when you're naked, and the bot tells you your score.

## Records and penalties

* Beat the record for your item count, or set the first one of the day, and you go free.
* Otherwise a **bondage penalty** locks on: 10 minutes, plus 2 more for each earlier attempt that day. Sometimes the bot offers a themed bondage set instead; answer yes or no.
* **Sensitive items are your call.** Before your first solo game the bot asks whether the penalty may include nipple clamps, a chastity belt or a tail plug (anything on the breasts, nipples, pelvis or butt). Say no and they're left out of every theme and outfit. Change it anytime with `/bot settings sensitive yes` or `/bot settings sensitive no`.

## Leaderboards

* `/bot scores` — both leaderboards. `/bot scores race` or `/bot scores survive` for one.
* `/bot scores me` — your own stats.
