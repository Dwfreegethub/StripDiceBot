# Matchmaking

Nobody in the room? Matchmaking lets players who want a game find each other.

1. **Register once:** `/bot bd register`. You also add the bot as a friend, which beeps need.
2. **When you want a game:** come to the room and type `/bot looking`. The bot beeps every registered player who's online.
3. **When someone else is looking**, you get a beep. Come over, or ignore it.

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/bot bd status` | Your current status |
| `/bot bd pause` · `/bot bd resume` | Stop or restart getting beeps; you stay registered |
| `/bot bd unregister` | Leave the pool |

**Be kind with `/bot looking`.** It has a cooldown, and players who call people over and then leave early get strikes. Too many strikes and you're taken out of the pool until an admin puts you back.
