# Tournament

Now and then an admin runs a **solo tournament** over several days. While one is on, the bot mentions it in `/bot help`.

* **Format:** Swiss pairing with double elimination. Each round you're matched with someone on a similar record. Lose twice and you're out.
* **Matches** are a few solo *survive* games each. You and your opponent play whenever you like during the round; you don't need to be online together.
* **Losing**, after the grace rounds, means time spent bound and claimable in the room. You can serve it in parts.

The exact settings (round length, games per match, punishment times) change from one tournament to the next. Type `/bot tournament rules` in the room for the current ones.

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/bot tournament` | Standings, your opponent, time left |
| `/bot tournament register` | Sign up while registration is open |
| `/bot tournament play` | Play one of your games for this round |
| `/bot tournament serve` · `/bot tournament stop` | Start or stop serving time you owe |
| `/bot tournament withdraw` | Leave (between rounds, if allowed) |

All the tournament commands are on the [Commands](Commands) page.
